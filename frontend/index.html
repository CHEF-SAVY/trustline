What stays private

On-chain, an attestation reveals only: your address, a hash of your XRPL address, a tier 0–3, an LTV, and two timestamps. Your transaction history, counterparties, balances and volumes never leave the TEE. The XRPL address itself is visible in the request event — the TEE has to be told what to score — so the borrower↔XRPL link is public while the evidence behind the score is not.
